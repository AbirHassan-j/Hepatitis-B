THE BIG QUESTION

How can something so tiny affect an entire organ?

Your body is built from tiny living units called cells. Cells work together to make tissues, tissues build organs, and organs work together in organ systems. In this mission, you will follow that path all the way to the liver.

One important thing to remember

Hepatitis B does not affect everyone in the same way. Some people clear the infection, while others develop a long-lasting infection. When infection becomes chronic, ongoing inflammation can damage liver tissue over time.

Meet your liver

Your liver is a busy organ that helps process nutrients, makes bile to help digest fats, stores energy, and breaks down many harmful substances.

A simplified illustration of the liver.

The body's multitasking station

The liver contains millions of liver cells called hepatocytes. They work together to carry out many important jobs.

🥗

Processes nutrients

Helps handle nutrients absorbed from food.

🧪

Makes bile

Bile helps your body digest fats.

⚡

Stores energy

Stores some energy for your body to use later.

🧹

Breaks down substances

Changes and removes many substances your body does not need.

Why study the liver?

Hepatitis B is a virus that can infect liver cells. The body's immune response helps fight infection, but inflammation can also injure liver tissue. Let's investigate how this works.
